About Alumni Association

The bridge that keeps the Daffodil family connected for life.

The DIU Alumni Portal is a dedicated online platform designed to strengthen the connection between Daffodil International University and its graduates. It serves as a central hub where alumni can reconnect with former classmates, stay informed about university news, and engage with the ever-growing DIU alumni community.

Our mission is to build a strong and supportive network that encourages lifelong relationships, professional collaboration, and knowledge sharing. Through this platform, alumni can explore career opportunities, participate in university events, celebrate achievements, and inspire current students by sharing their experiences and success stories.

The portal also provides easy access to alumni profiles, important announcements, networking opportunities, and community activities. Whether you are looking to reconnect with old friends, expand your professional network, or contribute to the growth of the DIU community, the DIU Alumni Portal is your gateway to staying connected.
